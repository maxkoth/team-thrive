Phase 3 · Athlete Journey

The same history,
seen three ways

Coach

Your team's record

Open any athlete on your team and see their tenure, join date, jersey number, and a season-by-season record — position, attendance, games, trainings and days out injured. You see your own team only.

Parent

The whole story

Open your child's profile and see every team they've been on — current and past — then open any team for the season-by-season detail. Parents see the complete record.

Athlete · 13+

Their own history

Athletes see their own record across every team they've been part of, with the same detail per team, plus their calendar and settings.

The record doesn't end when an athlete leaves your program — it goes with them. That's the point.

Phase 3 · Notes & Journal

Notes, with
clear rules

Coach

Write and share

You always see your own notes, and you see athlete or parent notes when they've been shared with your team. You can edit and delete your own.

Parent

Nothing hidden

Parents see everything on their child's profile — the coach's notes, the athlete's and their own — and choose, note by note, what the coach can see.

Athlete · 13+

Their own journal

Athletes read the coach's and their parent's notes, write their own, and choose per note whether the coach sees it. Their parent always can.

Nothing on a child's profile is ever hidden from their parent.

Phase 3 · AI Video

Upload a clip.
Get real feedback.

How it works

A parent uploads a short clip of their athlete — a vault, a routine, a skill. The AI returns coaching feedback on what it sees: approach, technique, execution. The feedback is saved to the athlete's profile and becomes part of their journey.

Where the lines are

Parents upload; athletes can view. Feedback is always labelled developmental — it is never presented as verified statistics, and it never replaces the coach. Athletes under 13 are not part of this.

We're building a working prototype first, so you can try it on real clips from your own gym and judge the quality before it goes into the product.
