What you're watching

The week of a club,
in one app

Set up the club

Create your club, add teams and coaches, and build rosters. Import athletes from another team instead of typing them in again.

Bring in families

One invite link and parents are in. Team messaging with read receipts, so "I told them" and "they know" finally mean the same thing.

Run the season

Practices, games and meets on one calendar, with attendance tracked and reminders that go out on their own.

Get paid

Connect your club's Stripe account once. Dues and competition fees are collected in-app and the money goes straight to you.

Keep the record

Every athlete builds a profile across the seasons they spend with you — the history that usually lives in a coach's memory.

Free to download

On iPhone and Android. Coaches, parents and athletes all use the same app, each seeing what they should.
